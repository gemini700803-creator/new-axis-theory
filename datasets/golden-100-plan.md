# Golden 100 Benchmark Plan

The purpose of this benchmark is to evaluate whether New Axis Theory can detect invalid questions, environmental variables, subjective questions, forced-answer situations, and high-risk reasoning failures better than standard LLM systems.

## Category Distribution

| Category | Count |
|-----------|--------|
| Environmental Variables | 20 |
| Verifiable Future Questions | 10 |
| Invalid Questions | 15 |
| Self-Contradictory Questions | 15 |
| Forced Choice Questions | 15 |
| Subjective Questions | 10 |
| Missing Context Questions | 10 |
| High-Risk Decision Questions | 5 |

Total: 100

## Evaluation Goals

- Detect invalid questions
- Detect environmental variables
- Avoid hallucination
- Avoid forced answering
- Preserve human decision power
- Provide lowest-risk response
- Improve verification awareness
